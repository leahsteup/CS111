Leah

I am an undergraduate student at Huntington University studying mathematics education with a minor in broadcasting. My goal is to be a secondary math teacher who freelances as a comedian and videographer. I hope to make a positive impact on the world in everything I do. My main goal is to help people!

My main hobby is writing and performing sketch, improv, and stand-up comedy. I write 5 jokes every morning to exercise my sense of humor--and demonstrate my dedication to the craft. I've also attended Brigham Young University's Sketch Comedy Workshop and taken several classes at Fort Wayne Improv. However, my favorite form of humor is writing (and performing) sketch comedy. You can find examples of my sketches on my YouTube channel Joyful Comedy.
My secondary hobby is videography. I am the Media Team Leader for Huntington University's Center for Spiritual Formation, which means I get to create fun videos and posts for their Instagram account. I also make video testimonies for Arise, a church in Roanoke, Indiana. Most of my video experience, however, comes from my work with school news programs. I was a reporter and anchor for HHS In-Depth, Homestead High School's weekly newscast. I've also been involved with FDN News, Huntington University's newscast, since fall 2025. I made a news story, called a package, for FDN News about a disc golf incident at HU, which won the Solo Storyteller award from the 2025 Great Lakes Emmy Chapter Student Production Awards.
