HU Ag Trip 2026 Documentary

Link to Video

One of my most recent video projects is a 5-minute documentary covering the Huntington University Department of Agriculture's mission trip to Momostenango, Guatemala, in March 2026. I traveled with the team to take photos and record video footage with a Lumix GH5 camera. I also interviewed each team member after the trip for a total of 10 interviews ranging from 5 to 30 minutes. I edited the final documentary on Adobe Premiere Pro.
